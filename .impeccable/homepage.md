# STILL homepage surface brief

Mode: Persuade. Accepted direction: The Quiet Author Design 188, Nordic Light Spa.

The implemented journey moves from a full-bleed promise and overlapping quick-planning strip through introduction, immersive spa and ritual articles, room tabs, dining and nature, three trip ideas, a five-image gallery, personal planner, FAQ, and closing invitation.

Priority actions: explore the atmosphere, select a room or interest, and save a personal travel draft. The fictional hotel status remains explicit. No prices, location, availability, or commercial proof are invented. A submitted planner creates a local summary and downloadable text file, not a booking.

The image set contains 15 distinct subjects: eight generated and seven licensed stock. Responsive derivatives are delivery sizes, not additional subjects. Gallery and room-tab reuse is intentional. The generated `breakfast-v2` image replaces the former stock breakfast: restrained ceramics and a croissant by the lake connect the dining imagery to STILL’s atmosphere. The main dining photograph spans the copy and breakfast rows on desktop, eliminating orphan whitespace.

Refinement motion supports specific surfaces: daylight across dining, water reflection over the pool, bounded dining depth, button sheen, a room-image transition, gallery hover lift, and a slim top reading-progress line. Ambient scenes pause offscreen and in hidden tabs; manual pause and reduced-motion preferences remain available.

GitHub is the sole source and publishing route after export. This brief records the implemented homepage; reusable visual rules belong in DESIGN.md.
