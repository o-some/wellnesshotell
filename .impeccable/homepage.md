# STILL homepage surface brief

Mode: Persuade. Accepted direction: The Quiet Author Design 188, Nordic Light Spa.

The implemented journey moves from a full-bleed promise and overlapping quick-planning strip through introduction, a dawn breathing chapter, immersive spa and alternating ritual rows, room tabs, a slow-living passage, dining, nature, a full evening pool scene, a fireside lounge scene, three trip ideas, a five-image gallery, personal planner, FAQ, and closing invitation.

Priority actions: explore the atmosphere, select a room or interest, and save a personal travel draft. The fictional hotel status remains explicit. No prices, location, availability, or commercial proof are invented. A submitted planner creates a local summary and downloadable text file, not a booking.

The image set contains 18 distinct subjects: eleven generated and seven licensed stock. Responsive derivatives are delivery sizes, not additional subjects. Gallery and room-tab reuse is intentional. Generated dawn and dusk landscapes frame the expanded day-long narrative; the generated fireside lounge adds an intimate final interior scene. Its 800px and 1440px derivatives support responsive delivery. The generated `breakfast-v2` image replaces the former stock breakfast: restrained ceramics and a croissant by the lake connect dining to STILL’s atmosphere. The main dining photograph spans the copy and breakfast rows on desktop, eliminating orphan whitespace.

The dawn chapter uses a 175svh desktop scroll region with a sticky 100svh stage, a bounded opening aperture, and small image/headline shifts. The expanded rituals alternate large photographs and longer copy; rooms and gallery also use larger imagery. Slow-living copy adds a quiet text-led pause before dining. Forest, evening pool, and fireside lounge form three consecutive full-bleed chapters before trip ideas. Dusk and fireside use a 96px overlap with a matching top gradient mask on desktop, reduced to 48px on mobile, so the scenes blend without blank seams.

Motion uses native scrolling and one requestAnimationFrame scroll clock: bounded dawn, ritual, dining, pool, nature, evening, and fireside depth; daylight across dining; water reflection over the pool; button sheen; room-image transitions; gallery hover lift; and a slim top reading-progress line. Ambient scenes pause offscreen and in hidden tabs. A fixed lower-right control pauses motion throughout the page and disables root smooth scrolling. Narrow screens use a normal-flow dawn scene with a small scroll-linked aperture and image shift. The four full-bleed scenes use parallax at 35% desktop strength; dusk and fireside also carry slow reflected-light pulses. Ritual images stay static on mobile. Existing hero/daylight ambience remains available. Reduced-motion preferences disable animation and transforms. Dusk captions sit 151px above the scene bottom on desktop and 115px on mobile. The fireside mobile footer also sits at 115px, while its 88% horizontal image framing keeps the fire visible.

GitHub is the sole source and publishing route after export. This brief records the implemented homepage; reusable visual rules belong in DESIGN.md.
