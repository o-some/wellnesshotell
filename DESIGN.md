# STILL — Nordic Light

Source direction: The Quiet Author Design 188, Nordic Light Spa. Kernel-selected and visually inspected; only abstract typographic, color and material principles transferred.

Visual contract: a quiet lakeside retreat, silver-blue water and warm limestone. Full-bleed landscape first; typography is editorial, left aligned, with generous breathing room. Cormorant Garamond provides softness and Manrope provides clarity. Foreground #24434b, paper #f6f5f0, water #dfe9e9, deep #173d47. Distinct identity comes from a split relationship between water and warm interiors, not generic luxury gold.

Surface mode: Persuade. Hero promise → immersive spa → room selection → dining/nature → trip packages → personal planner. Three priority actions: explore, select room, save travel draft. The concept is labeled, no invented commercial proof.

Shading supplies readable image overlays, floating planner depth and nested photographic depth in the introduction. Motion has four purposeful families, native scrolling and reduced-motion/pause support. Desktop parallax is deliberately disabled on narrow screens. Images remain visible when JS or motion is disabled.

Quality bar: a cohesive, calm hospitality editorial with a complete usable concept journey, all 15 distinct photos represented, no clipped German headings, no generic icon tiles. User delegated the design selection. No speculative aesthetic approval gate.
